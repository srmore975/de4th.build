De4th.build — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

TL;DR: De4th.build is a desktop application. It does not operate any servers and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything it does happens on your own computer or directly through Discord's API using your own bot.

1. What the App Is

De4th.build is a local desktop tool that you run on your own computer. It connects to Discord using a bot token that you create and provide. It also reads public YouTube RSS feeds to detect new uploads.

3. What We Collect From You

We do not collect any personal data. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics, no login, and no tracking. The App does not contain any network endpoint that receives data from you.

4. Where Your Data Goes

When you use the App, it communicates only with services you direct it to:

  • Discord API (discord.com) — to operate your bot and build your server. Subject to the Discord Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube public feeds (youtube.com) — to check for new uploads from the channel you specify. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

No third party, and no server we operate, ever receives your bot token or your settings.

5. Data You Configure the Bot to Post

If you enable the YouTube notifier, the App posts a message (video title, link, thumbnail, and optional role mention) into a Discord channel you choose. This message becomes visible to members of that server and is governed by Discord's systems, not by the App.

6. Third-Party Services

The App is not affiliated with Discord or YouTube. Your use of Discord and YouTube is subject to their respective Terms of Service and Privacy Policies. You should review them:
Discord Privacy Policy · Google/YouTube Privacy Policy

7. Data Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Deleting the App's settings file (stored in your user data folder, e.g. %APPDATA%\De4th.build on Windows);
  • Resetting your Discord bot token in the Discord Developer Portal; or
  • Uninstalling the App (note: some built apps leave a settings file behind — this file contains no personal data beyond your own server layout and the encrypted token placeholder).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your region) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Security

Your Discord bot token is encrypted at rest using your operating system's user-level encryption. It is decrypted only in memory, only while the App is running, and only sent to the Discord API. No logs of the token are written.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Effective date" at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the server owner or creator of your instance of the App.
